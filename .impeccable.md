## Design Context

### Users

The primary users are CrazyMarket players arriving at the game in a landscape
viewport with mouse, controller, keyboard, or touch input. Their immediate job
is to start or resume a run, inspect lightweight progress context, open options,
or quit without losing the lively supermarket atmosphere behind the UI.

### Brand Personality

Playful, tactile, and scrappy. The interface should feel physically grounded in
CrazyMarket's checkout world: expressive and a little imperfect, but never
chaotic, childish, or difficult to read.

### Aesthetic Direction

Use a warm off-white receipt emerging from a compact register/printer on the
right side of a centered landscape composition. Pair dark printed ink with a
rare checkout-yellow interaction accent, barcode/rule details, torn paper,
register metadata, and typography that feels stamped or thermally printed.
Avoid the prototype's pink theme, generic cards, glass effects, neon, and UI
that spreads important elements to the screen edges.

### Design Principles

- Keep every menu action readable, at least 44px tall, and equally operable by
  mouse, controller, keyboard, and touch.
- Make the receipt/printer metaphor structural: paper feed, printed hierarchy,
  barcode, register label, and torn edge should explain the composition rather
  than decorate it randomly.
- Preserve the live market as atmosphere without dimming it, while maintaining
  strong ink-on-paper contrast inside the interactive surface.
- Use motion to explain paper feeding and confirm presses; keep frequent hover
  and navigation feedback fast and restrained, with reduced-motion fallbacks.
- Center the composition within safe landscape bounds and adapt the receipt's
  scale/spacing before allowing any content to clip or become too small.

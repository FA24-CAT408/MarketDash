# Glossary

- **CinemachineCamera**: Cinemachine 3's concrete virtual-camera component.
- **Brain**: component on Main Camera that selects/blends active Cinemachine cameras.
- **Decollider**: built-in Cinemachine extension that moves a camera sphere out of obstacles.
- **Ground Guard**: custom Cinemachine extension enforcing final floor/ceiling clearance.
- **Surface Constraint**: custom MonoBehaviour that shortens orbit radius and limits pitch.
- **Selective Occluder**: marker whose renderers can be hidden without moving the camera.
- **Guided Rail**: spline-driven Cinemachine camera used by Guided and Hybrid modes.
